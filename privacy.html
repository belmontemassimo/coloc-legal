Privacy Policy

Coloc · Last updated: 16 October 2026

Coloc helps people who live together organise their shared home: a shopping list, tasks, chores, a calendar and a notes board. This policy explains what data the app processes, why, and the choices you have. The controller of your data is Massimo Belmonte, France, contact: belmontemassimax@gmail.com.

We do not use advertising, tracking, analytics SDKs, or sell your data. We do not access your contacts, location, camera or photos.

Who can see your data

Content you add to a flat is visible to the members of that flat only. Your name and avatar are visible to people you share a flat with. Anyone with your flat's invite code can join it, so only share the code with your flatmates; an admin can reset the code at any time in Settings.

Service providers

  • Supabase (database, authentication, hosting), servers in the European Union (Ireland).
  • Expo / Apple Push Notification service / Firebase Cloud Messaging to deliver push notifications (only the notification text and your device token are shared).

Where providers process data outside the EEA, transfers rely on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

How long we keep data

We keep your data while your account exists. Ticked shopping items are hidden from the list after 7 days and completed tasks after 14 days; they remain stored with the flat until a member deletes them or the flat is deleted. When you leave a flat, your tasks are unassigned and you are removed from chore rotations. When the last member leaves, the flat and all its content are deleted.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time in Settings → Delete account. This immediately and permanently deletes your account, profile and device tokens, and removes you from every flat. Content you created in a shared flat (for example a note) is deleted with the flat when its last member leaves; you can delete your own notes and items before leaving.

Your rights

You can access, correct, export or delete your data, object to or restrict processing, and withdraw consent. Contact us at belmontemassimax@gmail.com. You may also complain to your data protection authority (in France, the CNIL).

Children

Coloc is intended for people aged 16 and over.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Access to each flat's data is enforced by the database itself (row-level security), not just by the app.

Changes

We will update this page and the date above if this policy changes, and tell you in the app if the changes are significant.
