Coloc support

Flat life, sorted.

Coloc keeps your flatshare organised: a shared shopping list, tasks, chores that rotate fairly, a calendar with activities, and a notes board.

Contact

Questions, bugs or ideas: belmontemassimax@gmail.com. We usually reply within two working days.

I forgot my password

Coloc doesn't send emails, so password resets are done by hand. Email us from the address you signed up with and we'll send you a temporary password. Then change it in Settings → Change password.

How do flatmates join?

In Settings, the invite card shows your flat's 8-character code. Share it (or the invite link) with your flatmates; they enter it after creating their account. An admin can reset the code at any time.

Leaving a flat or deleting my account

Settings → Leave this flat removes you from one flat. Settings → Delete account permanently deletes your account and removes you from every flat.

Notifications

Coloc notifies you when a task is assigned to you, a flatmate plans something, a note is posted, someone joins, or it's your turn for a chore. You can turn them off in your phone's settings.
